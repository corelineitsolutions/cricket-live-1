Purpose:
Test live-match answers, IPL history, ICC event history, player statistics, venue analysis, predictions, English/Hindi/Hinglish understanding, follow-up context, and safe handling of missing data.

Instructions:
- Ask these questions one by one to the AI.
- For live-match questions, use an actually live match and verify against the provider/API.
- Questions mentioning a specific year or tournament should be checked against the correct edition.
- Do not expect the AI to invent live scores or missing historical facts.
- Predictions should be identified as estimates, not certainties.

PASS CRITERIA
1. Correct intent is identified.
2. The correct player, team, tournament, season, venue, and format are used.
3. Historical facts match the database or a trusted reference.
4. Live answers use fresh match data and identify when data is unavailable or stale.
5. Hindi and Hinglish versions preserve the meaning of the English question.
6. Follow-up questions use context only when the reference is clear.
7. Predictions are labelled as estimates and do not promise certainty.
8. The AI does not invent scores, records, citations, or database results.
9. Statistical comparisons state the relevant format, time period, and sample size where possible.
10. Ambiguous questions trigger a clarification instead of an unsupported guess.
